TaskRows Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026. Last updated: 24 November 2026.

TaskRows is a Windows desktop application that displays and edits your Google Tasks lists in rows on a tall (portrait) monitor. It is open source and runs entirely on your own computer. There is no TaskRows server, no account with TaskRows, and no TaskRows database. This policy describes exactly what data the application touches, where it is stored, and how to remove it.

1. Who operates TaskRows

TaskRows is published by its developer at github.com/tumngobTH/taskrows. You install it yourself and connect it to Google using an OAuth client created in your own Google Cloud project. The developer never receives, sees, or can access your Google data.

2. Information TaskRows accesses

3. How the information is used

  • Task data is shown on screen and edited according to your actions. Edits you make are sent back to Google Tasks through the Google Tasks API so they appear on your other devices.
  • Your email address is shown next to your account in the app's account panel. It is not used for anything else.
  • TaskRows does not use your data for advertising, analytics, profiling, model training, or any purpose other than operating the task board for you.

4. Where the information is stored

  • Task cache: a copy of your lists and tasks is saved on your computer in %APPDATA%\com.kim.taskrows\cache.json so the app can start instantly and work offline. Account labels (email, colour) are saved in %APPDATA%\com.kim.taskrows\accounts.json.
  • Credentials: OAuth refresh tokens, and the OAuth client ID and secret you entered, are stored in Windows Credential Manager under the service name taskrows. Short-lived access tokens are held in memory only and discarded when the app closes.
  • Nothing is stored anywhere other than your own computer and your own Google account.

5. Sharing and transfers

TaskRows communicates only with Google's servers (oauth2.googleapis.com, openidconnect.googleapis.com, tasks.googleapis.com). It does not transmit any data to the developer or to any third party, and contains no telemetry, crash reporting, or analytics of any kind. Your data is never sold, rented, or shared.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

TaskRows' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular, Google user data is used only to provide and improve the user-facing features of TaskRows, is not transferred to others, is not used for advertising, and is never read by humans other than you.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Signing out an account inside TaskRows deletes that account's refresh token from Credential Manager and removes its lists from the cache.
  • Uninstalling TaskRows removes the application. You can delete %APPDATA%\com.kim.taskrows\ to remove the cache, and remove the taskrows entries in Windows Credential Manager to remove all stored credentials.
  • You can revoke TaskRows' access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After revocation TaskRows can no longer read or change your tasks.
  • Because the developer holds no copy of your data, there is nothing for the developer to delete.

8. Security

TaskRows signs in using OAuth 2.0 with PKCE and a loopback redirect, never asks for your Google password, and stores long-lived tokens only in the operating system's credential store. All traffic to Google uses HTTPS.

9. Children

TaskRows is a general productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13.

10. Changes to this policy

Changes are published at this address with an updated date. The full edit history is available in the repository.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or about your data: open an issue at github.com/tumngobTH/taskrows/issues, or write to the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in consent screen.
